# Sources

## Paimon (`paimon`) and Yelan (`yelan`)

Both spritesheets were generated from single **transparent character portraits**
downloaded from [Project Amber / gi.yatta.moe](https://gi.yatta.moe):

- Paimon: `UI_AvatarIcon_Paimon.png`
- Yelan: `UI_AvatarIcon_Yelan.png`

The portraits themselves are **not** committed here to avoid redistributing
miHoYo/HoYoverse art without permission. The spritesheets under `pets/` are
derived fan works for personal use. Re-run `route-b.js` with your own portrait
to regenerate.
